Studies & Credits

fudami stands on the shoulders of giants. We are deeply grateful to the researchers and open-source projects that make this platform possible.

  • science Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS)

    The core scheduling algorithm is based on FSRS, a state-of-the-art spaced repetition algorithm that significantly outperforms SM-2. Research by open-spaced-repetition.

  • draw KanjiVG

    Kanji stroke order data and SVG animations are powered by KanjiVG, an open-source database of Japanese kanji stroke diagrams created by Ulrich Apel.

  • translate JMdict & KANJIDIC

    This publication has included material from the JMdict and KANJIDIC2 dictionary files in accordance with the licence provisions of the Electronic Dictionaries Research Group. See www.edrdg.org (CC BY-SA).

  • format_quote Tatoeba

    Example sentences come from the Tatoeba project (CC BY 2.0 FR).

  • school JLPT levels

    Word and kanji levels come from Jonathan Waller's JLPT resources (CC BY), mapped to JMdict by yomitan-jlpt-vocab and kanji-data. There is no official list: treat them as estimates.

  • record_voice_over Pitch accent & furigana

    Pitch accent from Kanjium and furigana from JmdictFurigana (CC BY-SA).

  • text_fields Typography

    Plus Jakarta Sans (SIL Open Font License) and Material Symbols (Apache 2.0), served from our own sites; Japanese text uses your device's own fonts. Kanji stroke order in the app uses the KanjiStrokeOrders font.
