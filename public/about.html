About fudami

Anki schedules reviews better than almost anything else, and many people stop using it. Duolingo is easy to come back to every day, and lighter on long-term memory. fudami puts the scheduling (FSRS) inside the daily habit, for Japanese, starting from the first kana.

Who builds it

Arno builds fudami alone. The code of this site is public, on GitHub.

What fudami will not do

  • Show ads. There are none, and none are planned.
  • Take lives or hearts away: a wrong answer never stops you studying.
  • Invent urgency: no fake countdowns, and every reminder can be switched off in one tap.
  • Sell or share your data. The privacy page lists everything we keep and for how long.
