Privacy & Cookies

We collect as little as possible, use no third-party tracking, and never sell your data. Here is exactly what happens.

Last updated: 23 June 2026

Who we are

fudami is an independent Japanese-learning project. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@fudami.net. This policy covers the marketing site (fudami.net) and the app (app.fudami.net).

What we collect

  • Account details — if you sign in, our authentication provider (Clerk) processes your email and basic profile from Google or Apple. We use it only to create and secure your account.
  • Learning progress — once the app launches, your reviews are tied to your account ID so we can show your progress. No payment or sensitive special-category data is collected.
  • Technical data — like any website, our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address and request metadata to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Language requests — if you vote in the “request a language” poll, we record that one vote per language using a short-lived per-IP marker to prevent duplicates. We do not build a profile from it.

Cookies & local storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no cross-site trackers. What we do use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — security cookies set by Cloudflare to keep the site available, and, only after you sign in, session cookies set by Clerk to keep you logged in.
  • Functional local storage — your theme (light/dark), language, and language-poll choices are saved in your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Because we use only strictly-necessary and functional storage, we don’t show a tracking-consent banner. If we ever add analytics, we’ll ask first.

Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, and security (see Cloudflare’s privacy policy).
  • Clerk — authentication and session management (see Clerk’s privacy policy).
  • Google / Apple — only if you choose them to sign in.

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Retention

Account data lasts while your account exists; delete your account and we delete it. The poll’s per-IP marker expires automatically within 24 hours. Security logs are kept only as long as needed to protect the service.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR), you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to certain processing. Email privacy@fudami.net and we’ll respond. fudami is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We’ll update this page if our practices change and revise the “last updated” date above.
